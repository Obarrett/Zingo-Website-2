# Zingo — Build Brief Addendum: "The People Behind It" Section (Revision)

A revision brief for an existing homepage section. All copy below is final unless flagged. Visual design decisions remain the implementer's, but this brief includes required corrections to the current build.

---

## Context

This section currently exists on the page as a two-column layout: credentials on the left, a testimonial card on the right. The two-column structure is working and should be kept. The copy and the right-hand card are being replaced.

## Required corrections to the current build

1. **Spelling: "Juilliard" is currently misspelled as "Juilliard" (extra L) in both the subhead and the badge.** The correct spelling is J-u-i-l-l-i-a-r-d: one L before the i, two before the second i — "Juilliard". This must be verified character by character anywhere the word appears. A misspelled credential undermines the entire section.
2. **Remove the testimonial quote entirely** ("The stuff I wish I'd had my first year teaching..."). It is not a real attributed quote and cannot appear on the site. It is replaced by the "Why we built it" card below.
3. **Remove the "Built with directors / Tested in real beginning bands" footer** from the card. These claims are not yet substantiated.
4. **No em-dashes anywhere in this section** (or sitewide). Replace with commas, periods, or restructure.

More than half the kids who start band never make it to high school. Not because they can't. Because beginner teaching hasn't changed in fifty years.

## Functional and layout notes

- Keep the two-column structure: credentials left, card right.
- "So we're changing it." is intentionally its own short paragraph and should read as the closing beat of the card. Give it visual weight or breathing room.
- The previous "Classroom educators" badge is retired; the subhead now carries the classroom credential. Badge row is exactly three items.
- The card's avatar/footer slot from the previous design is removed for now. The card is mission-voiced, not attributed.
- This card is a placeholder owner for a future real, attributed director testimonial. When one exists (with written permission), the testimonial takes over the card and the "Why we built it" copy folds into the left column. Build the card so this swap is straightforward.

## Flags (for the record)

- "More than half the kids who start band never make it to high school" is the same claim family as the hero's 55% stat and shares its citation requirement before launch.
- "Hasn't changed in fifty years" is rhetorical. If a softer version is ever needed: "hasn't kept up with how kids actually learn." Do not change without instruction; noted here only as the approved fallback.
