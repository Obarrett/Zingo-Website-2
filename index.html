The people behind it

Built bymusicians.

Zingo was founded by a music educator and a software engineer who believed students stick with music when practice feels engaging and rewarding.

Our music founder is a Juilliard-trained educator who has worked in classrooms across the U.S. and abroad, and with the education departments of Carnegie Hall and the New York Philharmonic. He saw the same pattern everywhere: students left rehearsal motivated, but unsure how to practice on their own.

So he partnered with a software engineer and former startup founder who knew how to turn ideas into products. Together, they built Zingo to keep students playing.

Institutional names reflect the founders' personal backgrounds, not endorsement or partnership.
